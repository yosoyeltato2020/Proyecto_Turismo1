01

Luxury Rooms

Spacious rooms designed to provide comfort, rest and a relaxing atmosphere.

The hotel offers elegant rooms and suites, comfortable beds, bathrooms, television and Wi-Fi access.

Spalace Wellness Center offers a hydrothermal circuit, massages and beauty treatments in a 1,500 m² spa. Access and treatments may require a separate reservation or payment.
