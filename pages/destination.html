TOLEDO

A CITY FULL OF HISTORY

Discover one of Spain's most beautiful historic cities.

Toledo is a city rich in history, culture and architectural heritage. Its streets and monuments reflect the influence of different civilisations and traditions.

A city with a unique heritage

Known as the City of Three Cultures, Toledo preserves Christian, Jewish and Muslim influences in its historic centre.

Visitors can admire its monuments, discover its artistic heritage and enjoy traditional local food.
